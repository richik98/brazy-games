3. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run the Games, keep them stable, show ads where a Game is ad-supported, process purchases, and answer you. We do not require a real name or home address to play.

3.1 You give us

  • Support messages: email address, name if you include it, and whatever you write (device model, screenshots, player/game ID, purchase receipts).
  • Optional profile details in Games that have them (for example a nickname or avatar).
  • Feedback, surveys, or beta notes if you choose to send them.

We do not ask for payment card numbers. See Payments.

3.2 Collected automatically in the Games

Depending on the Game, platform, and your consent settings, we and our service providers may collect:

  • Device and log data: IP address, device type, OS version, language, country/region, time zone, app version, crash logs, performance data.
  • Identifiers: advertising ID (GAID / IDFA, where available), app instance ID, and similar device or installation IDs.
  • Usage data: session length, levels played, scores, settings, feature use, approximate progress, and whether you watched an ad or made a purchase.
  • Approximate location: derived from IP or provided by the ad/attribution SDK. We do not need GPS for gameplay and we do not ask for precise location.
  • Diagnostics: crash reports and technical errors, so we can fix the Game.

3.3 From others

  • Google Play and Apple (install source, purchase validation, refunds, store account tokens as needed to deliver IAP).
  • Sign-in providers, only if you use them in a Game (for example Google or Apple). We may receive a display name and a provider user ID.
  • Advertising, mediation, attribution, and analytics partners (campaign performance, fraud signals, ad events).

We do not scrape your contacts, photos, microphone, or camera unless a specific Game asks for a permission and you grant it. Current Games do not need those permissions for core play.

4. How we use information

  • Provide, operate, update, and personalize the Games.
  • Save progress, settings, and (where the Game has them) online features such as battles or leaderboards.
  • Process and restore in-app purchases.
  • Measure what works, fix bugs, and improve design and balance.
  • Show ads in ad-supported Games, including measuring fills and preventing invalid traffic.
  • Attribute installs to ads and campaigns.
  • Detect cheating, fraud, abuse, and security issues.
  • Reply to support and legal requests.
  • Comply with tax, accounting, and store rules.

We do not use your data to train public AI models. We do not sell your personal data.

5. Legal bases (EEA / UK)

If GDPR / UK GDPR applies, we rely on:

  • Contract — to give you the Game, support, and purchases you asked for.
  • Legitimate interests — security, fraud prevention, basic analytics, product improvement, storing support mail. We balance this against your rights.
  • Consent — personalized advertising, certain analytics/SDK uses, and iOS tracking (App Tracking Transparency). You can refuse or withdraw consent without losing the Game; you may still see contextual ads.
  • Legal obligation — records we must keep (for example transaction evidence).

6. How we share information

We share data with:

  • Processors who host, store, or help us run the Games (cloud hosting, crash reporting, email).
  • Advertising, mediation, and attribution partners in ad-supported Games — typically device identifiers, approximate location, IP, and ad/app events. They act as independent controllers for their own advertising, under their own policies.
  • Analytics partners — aggregated or pseudonymous usage and performance data.
  • Google and Apple — to distribute the Games and process payments.
  • Professional advisers (legal, accounting) under confidentiality, when needed.
  • Authorities if the law requires it or to protect rights, safety, or the Games.
  • A buyer if we sell or merge the studio or a Game; we would require the buyer to respect this policy or notify you.

Third parties that receive data from the Games must protect it at least to the standard described here and required by Google Play and Apple, and may use it only as needed to provide their service to us — unless they are independent controllers (ads/attribution), in which case their policy also applies.

Partners change over time. The current set for a title is reflected in that app’s Data safety / App Privacy labels. We do not sell personal information as “sale” is commonly understood (money for a list of players).

7. Advertising, analytics, and tracking

Some Games contain ads and use mediation. SDKs may collect advertising IDs and usage signals to serve and measure ads (personalized where allowed, otherwise non-personalized / contextual).

You can limit this:

  • EEA / UK: consent / privacy form shown in the Game (where required). You can change your mind later in the Game’s settings if offered, or by writing to us.
  • iOS: we honor App Tracking Transparency. If you tap “Ask App Not to Track”, we do not access IDFA for tracking.
  • Android: reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ads personalization, in device settings.
  • Limit ad tracking / opt out of interest-based ads in Google or Apple account settings.

Analytics and crash tools help us see if a level is broken or the app crashes. They are not used to identify you in the real world.

Push notifications, if a Game uses them, are optional. You can disable them in the OS settings.

8. Payments

In-app purchases go through Google Play Billing or Apple In-App Purchase. We receive confirmation that a product was bought (and enough to restore it). We do not receive your full card number, bank details, or Apple/Google password.

Refunds are handled by the store under their rules.

9. This website

The studio site is static. We do not run accounts, marketing pixels, or ads on it.

  • If you use the contact form or email us, we get the name, address, and message you send, and use them only to reply.
  • Our host (Cloudflare) may keep short-term server logs (IP, user-agent, URL) for security and delivery. We do not use those logs to build advertising profiles.

The site does not set advertising cookies. Strictly necessary cookies may be used by the host for security and load balancing.

10. Retention and deletion

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above, or longer if the law requires it.

  • On-device progress lives on your phone. Uninstalling the Game usually deletes it, unless the Game uses a cloud save or store backup you enabled.
  • Server-side player data (if a Game has online features) is kept while the Game is live and for a reasonable period after last activity, then deleted or aggregated.
  • Support email is typically kept up to 24 months after the last reply, unless we must keep it longer (dispute, legal hold).
  • Purchase records may be kept for the statutory accounting period.
  • Ad / analytics data is retained according to our partners’ standard periods, usually in aggregated or identifier form.

To request deletion of personal data we hold, email apps@brazy.gg from the address you used, and tell us the Game and (if you have it) a player ID. We will process the request within 30 days unless the law allows more time. Some information we cannot delete (already-aggregated analytics, records we must keep, data only on your device).

Resetting your advertising ID in system settings stops future link-up of that ID; it does not uninstall the Game.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures: encryption in transit (HTTPS / TLS), access limited to people who need it, and hosting with reputable providers. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a breach that must be notified, we will do so as required by law.

12. International transfers

We are based in Portugal. Partners (stores, cloud, ads, analytics) may process data in other countries, including the United States. Where GDPR requires a safeguard, we rely on adequacy decisions and/or Standard Contractual Clauses, plus the partner’s own transfer tools.

13. Children

Our Games are rated for a general audience (PEGI 3 / Everyone where applicable). They are not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where that is the local digital-consent age), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children in that age group.

We do not ask players for a date of birth, school, or parent email as part of normal play. If you are a parent or guardian and think a child has sent us personal data, write to apps@brazy.gg and we will delete it.

Ad-supported Games use age-appropriate advertising setups required by the stores and ad partners (including non-personalized ads where child-directed treatment is required). If a specific Game is ever enrolled in a kids program, its store listing and this policy will say so.

14. Your rights

Depending on where you live (in particular the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), you may be able to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct it;
  • delete it;
  • restrict or object to certain processing;
  • receive a copy (portability);
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing that already happened;
  • opt out of personalized ads as described in section 7.

Email apps@brazy.gg. We may need to verify the request (for example by asking which Game and device you use). You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority. In Portugal that is the CNPD. In the UK, the ICO. You may use the authority in your country of residence.

15. Additional US state rights

If you are in California or another US state with a consumer privacy law, you may have rights to know, delete, correct, and opt out of “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

We do not sell personal information for money. Ad-supported Games may share identifiers with ad partners for advertising as described above. You can opt out via device / ATT / ads settings, and by emailing us. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes that require a separate right-to-limit notice under the CPRA.

16. Changes

We may update this policy. The “last updated” date at the top will change. For material changes we will post the new version here and, where appropriate, show an in-Game notice. Continued use of the Games or website after the update means the new policy applies.
